Overview. Fiscal 2025 revenue grew 9.2% to $4.8 billion, driven by a continued mix
shift toward our cloud platform, which now represents 61% of total revenue.
Operating margin expanded 110 basis points to 23.4% as cloud scale efficiencies
more than offset elevated AI-infrastructure depreciation.

Liquidity and Capital Resources. As of December 31, 2025, the Company held $1.2
billion in cash and cash equivalents and maintained an undrawn $500 million
revolving credit facility. Net leverage was 2.1x trailing-twelve-month EBITDA,
within the 3.5x covenant threshold. Management believes existing liquidity, cash
generated from operations, and available credit are sufficient to fund operations
for at least the next twelve months.

Outlook. For fiscal 2026, we expect revenue growth of 8% to 10% and operating
margin expansion of approximately 120 basis points. We expect free cash flow
conversion above 90% and intend to resume share repurchases in the second half of
the year, prioritizing deleveraging until net leverage falls below 1.5x.

Risk Factors (summary). A prolonged rise in interest rates could increase borrowing
costs and extend sales cycles for rate-sensitive customers in financial services.
Approximately 40% of revenue is tied to discretionary enterprise IT spending, which
is sensitive to macroeconomic cycles. A stronger U.S. dollar represents a 2 to 3
percentage point headwind to reported revenue.
