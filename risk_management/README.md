# Risk Management (BOX 4)

Introduced as a completely independent risk controls layer, separating portfolio optimization and margin protection from raw execution.

## Target Structure & Subdirectories

- **`portfolio/`**: Position sizing, joint distribution estimation, and correlation analysis.
- **`enterprise/`**: Value-at-Risk (VaR), Conditional VaR (CVaR), drawdown protections, and margin caps.
- **`agents/`**: Risk-auditor agents capable of intercepting and rejecting trade signals.

## Purpose & Architectural Rule

This box enforces the safety boundaries of the system. All trading signals generated by agent swarms must pass through the `risk_management` layer before going to live or paper execution.
